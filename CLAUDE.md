- Accounts: Meta 3120245488194717 WingstopUK-LPH (Translate Culture, Mas: boosts day-to-day organic), Meta 1339656694773714 WINGSTOP UK - MSQ (SMARTS MSQ), TikTok 7031642344902426625 WINGSTOPUK (Translate Culture monthly budget). MSQ's TikTok account is not connected yet: add it to `TIKTOK_ACCOUNTS` in `lib/paid.ts` once Diri has access, and the head to head picks it up.
- Agency comes from the account, unless the campaign name starts `MSQ` or `TC |` (`agencyOf`).
- Table `ads_daily` (day, platform, ad_id, placement), view `ads_by_day` (security_invoker). `lib/adsSync.ts` upserts the last 7 days in the intel sync. `loadPaid` sends ads to the page packed (`packAds`/`unpackAds`: ad details once, rows as arrays, non-Instagram Meta placements merged) because raw rows were ~7 MB. Backfilled 17 Jun to 20 Oct 2026; 90-day spend checked against Windsor to within £20 per account.
- Instagram post-level views and likes are organic only (Meta docs). Instagram account-level daily views include ad views: verified 20 Oct, they track IG ad impressions almost one for one. `igPaidModel` fits account views against IG ad impressions (k capped at 1, needs 21+ days, 10+ ad days, correlation 0.6+) and takes paid out; with no fit, ads are added on top.
- Instagram boosts run as their own ad post with a different permalink. Meta ads now also pull `source_instagram_media_id`; `adsSync` maps it to the organic post's `comment_key` (media.id = `ig_<id>`), so a boost and its post line up (backfilled 21 Oct: 17 of 32 boosted ads resolved, the rest point at posts older than the media table). Instagram's app shows a boosted reel's views including the ad; the API's post views are organic only.
- TikTok account plays include Spark Ad plays (every TikTok ad so far is Spark), so organic = plays minus Spark plays. TikTok paid follows come from the ad account; Meta reports no Instagram follows from ads, so Instagram follows can't be split.
- The Social switch covers every topline (`splitAdjust` in `lib/paid.ts`). Instagram account shares and saves don't move with ad shares or saves (checked 20 Oct: correlation about 0 over 60 days), so they are treated as organic and ad shares/saves are added for combined. TikTok Spark shares sit inside the account figure and are taken out. TikTok saves come per video with no paid split (left out of Paid). No platform reports reposts from ads. The Social hero reads organic first, then paid on top (spend, views, shares, saves); the Sprout sentence is labelled as all channels, paid included.

## Apify (free plan, $5 a month credit; APIFY_TOKEN in Vercel)
- Driven by the sync (`lib/apifyJobs.ts`): each sync collects finished runs, then starts due ones. Runs logged in `apify_runs` (cost in `usd`).
- Cadence (`lib/apifyJobs.ts`): Flavour Club hashtag hunt every ~46h on TikTok (clockworks, hashtags) and Instagram (`apify/instagram-hashtag-scraper`) into `fc_entries`; competitor TikTok and UK trends weekly (~160h). Daily runs need a paid Apify plan.
- `clockworks/tiktok-scraper`: latest 12 videos for `TIKTOK_HANDLES` in `lib/tiktok.ts` (wingstopuk, kfc_uki, nandosuk, mcdonaldsuk, popeyesuk, daveshotchickenuk, raisingcanes, raisingcanes_uk). Gives plays, likes, comments, shares, saves. About $0.32 a run (~$0.007 a video). Note: TikTok "kfcuki" is an unrelated fan account; KFC is kfc_uki. Slim Chickens has no UK TikTok we could find. raisingcanes_uk is unverified.
- `data_xplorer/tiktok-trends`: top 40 UK hashtags, 7 days. ~$0.10 a run. TikTok no longer exposes the UK sounds chart to these tools.
- `/api/apify/probe` is a test route guarded by a short-lived key in `ops_keys` (insert one with an expiry; never commit it). `?only=tiktok&input=<json>` tests an actor, `?run=jobs` runs the step, `?abort=<runId>` stops a run. It returns 400 unless `only` is given (it used to run every actor by default). `?ads=last_20dT` or `?ads=YYYY-MM-DD..YYYY-MM-DD` backfills ads (keep ranges to about 20 days to stay inside 60s); `?geocode=stores` places new stores.
- `apify/instagram-scraper` was too slow to start inside the 60s limit for profiles, but reads a single post URL in ~4s at ~$0: used by the entries-by-link box.
- Instagram hashtag hunt is blind (checked 28 Oct): logged-out Instagram returns only a hashtag's single top post, so `apify/instagram-hashtag-scraper` got 1 item (our own post) on every run. Instagram entries come in by link: `/api/flavour/entries` POST (Flavour Club sign-in), box in the Entries section; entries marked `hashtag = 'team'` always show and the hunt keeps that marker. Windsor's Sprout connector gives only our own published posts (no Smart Inbox), and Windsor's instagram connector has no tags or hashtag search. Automatic Instagram entries need either Sprout's own API (Messages endpoint, token in Vercel as SPROUT_API_TOKEN, plan must include API access) or our own Meta app (`/{ig-user-id}/tags`; hashtag search needs app review).
- `/api/apify/probe?dataset=<runId>` reads a finished run's raw results (free).
